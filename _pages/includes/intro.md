<h2 id="about-heading">About</h2>

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at **Iowa State University**, advised by [Prof. Yang Li](https://jerryyangli.github.io/). I received my M.S. and B.Eng. in Computer Science from **Zhejiang University**, where I worked with [Prof. Zhou Zhao](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=IIoFY90AAAAJ&hl=en).

My current research focuses on **efficient multimodal models**, with an emphasis on **video large language models (Video LLMs)**. I develop methods to improve their computational efficiency while preserving video understanding capabilities.

My broader research interests include **3D vision and embodied AI**, building on my prior work in 3D scene understanding and robotic manipulation.
